AI and automation for Renewable Energy

Smart Logics builds analytics and automation for renewable energy operators: performance monitoring across sites, fault detection from SCADA and sensor data, drone and image inspection for panels and blades, and reporting for owners and lenders. The aim is more generation from existing assets and fewer wasted site visits.

What can Smart Logics offer solar and wind developers, asset owners and O&M providers?

Performance and fault analytics

Models on inverter, SCADA and weather data that find underperformance and likely faults.

Drone and image inspection

Computer vision on drone or camera images to find hotspots, soiling, cracks and damage.

Forecasting and scheduling

Generation forecasts and maintenance scheduling around weather and demand.

Owner and lender reporting

Automated performance and availability reports from site data.

How does automation increase revenue?

  • Recovered generation from faster fault detection.
  • Better forecasts improve market and offtake outcomes.
  • Credible reports support financing and asset sales.

How much working time can it save?

  • Engineers stop compiling reports across sites.
  • Image review is largely automated.
  • Fewer site visits for faults that can be diagnosed remotely.

Hours saved are roughly tasks per month × minutes per task × share of tasks automated. We fill that in with your own numbers during discovery, so the estimate comes from your operation and not from a generic claim.

Where does the efficiency gain come from?

  • Underperformance is found sooner.
  • Maintenance is planned by impact.
  • One view across a mixed fleet of sites.

How do we measure success?

We agree a baseline before building, then track:

  • Availability and performance ratio
  • Mean time to detect and repair a fault
  • Inspection hours per megawatt
  • Reporting hours per month

How does a project with Smart Logics work?

  1. Discovery. We map your workflow, data and systems, and agree what success means.
  2. Architecture and pilot plan. We choose the smallest useful first release and how it connects to your tools.
  3. Sprint delivery. Working software every sprint, tested on your real data.
  4. Release and iteration. Monitoring, quality checks and improvements after launch.

Model accuracy depends on data quality and site conditions, and is validated on your own sites before relying on it.

Common questions

What data do you need to start?

Typically SCADA or inverter exports, weather data, maintenance logs and some sample inspection images. We check what exists during discovery.

Do you work with mixed equipment vendors?

Yes, though data formats differ, so integration effort varies. We assess this early.

Is this only for new projects?

No. Many gains come from existing operating assets where data is already being collected.
