AI and automation for Finance

Smart Logics automates document-heavy finance work: reading invoices, statements and KYC files, matching transactions, drafting reports and flagging exceptions for a person to decide. The systems are built around audit trails and access control, because in finance every automated step has to be explainable.

What can Smart Logics offer banks, lenders, fintechs and corporate finance teams?

Document intelligence

Extraction from invoices, bank statements, contracts and ID documents into structured fields with confidence scores.

Reconciliation and exception handling

Matching across ledgers, bank feeds and payment providers, with unmatched items queued for review.

KYC and onboarding workflows

Checks, completeness validation and routing that shorten the time from application to decision.

Reporting assistants

Drafts management commentary and answers finance questions from approved data sources.

How does automation increase revenue?

  • Faster onboarding means fewer applicants drop out.
  • Better collections improve cash flow.
  • Analysts have time for advisory and growth work.

How much working time can it save?

  • Finance staff spend less time on keying and matching.
  • Month-end close needs fewer manual passes.
  • Compliance teams review exceptions, not every file.

Hours saved are roughly tasks per month × minutes per task × share of tasks automated. We fill that in with your own numbers during discovery, so the estimate comes from your operation and not from a generic claim.

Where does the efficiency gain come from?

  • Consistent rules applied to every case.
  • Full logs of what was automated and who approved it.
  • Fewer errors passed downstream.

How do we measure success?

We agree a baseline before building, then track:

  • Days to close and days sales outstanding
  • Straight-through processing rate
  • Onboarding time from application to decision
  • Exception rate and rework hours

Common questions

Can this run inside our own cloud or on-premises?

Where policy requires it, yes. Deployment options are discussed in discovery, including private hosting and model choices.

How do you handle regulators and audits?

Every automated action is logged with inputs, outputs and the approver. We help you document the controls, but compliance decisions stay with your risk and legal teams.

Do humans stay in the loop?

For decisions with financial or regulatory impact, yes. Automation prepares and checks; a person approves.
