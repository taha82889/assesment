How does automation increase revenue?

  • New paid AI features and higher-tier plans.
  • Better activation and retention from guided onboarding.
  • Faster shipping of roadmap items that customers are asking for.

How much working time can it save?

  • Support and CS teams handle fewer repetitive questions.
  • Engineers are not pulled off the roadmap to prototype AI.
  • Product managers spend less time on manual research and summaries.

Hours saved are roughly tasks per month × minutes per task × share of tasks automated. We fill that in with your own numbers during discovery, so the estimate comes from your operation and not from a generic claim.

Where does the efficiency gain come from?

  • A repeatable path from prototype to production.
  • Predictable model spend.
  • Quality that is measured instead of assumed.

How do we measure success?

We agree a baseline before building, then track:

  • Activation rate and time to first value
  • Support tickets per active account
  • AI feature adoption and retention of adopters
  • Cost per AI request and answer quality score

How does a project with Smart Logics work?

  1. Discovery. We map your workflow, data and systems, and agree what success means.
  2. Architecture and pilot plan. We choose the smallest useful first release and how it connects to your tools.
  3. Sprint delivery. Working software every sprint, tested on your real data.
  4. Release and iteration. Monitoring, quality checks and improvements after launch.

Security reviews, data processing terms and model-provider choices are agreed before any customer data touches an AI model.

Common questions

Do you work inside our codebase and tools?

Yes. We work in your repository, ticketing and CI process, and follow your coding standards and review rules.

Who owns the code and the prompts?

That is set out in the contract. Our default expectation is that you own the work product built for you; we agree it in writing before starting.

How do you keep customer data separate between tenants?

Tenant isolation is designed into retrieval and storage from the first sprint, and we test for cross-tenant leakage before release.
