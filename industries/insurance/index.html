Smart Logics automates document-heavy insurance work: reading submissions, claim forms, medical and repair reports, summarising them for underwriters and adjusters, flagging missing information and risk signals, and routing cases. People stay responsible for decisions on cover, pricing and payment.

How does automation increase revenue?

  • Underwriters quote more submissions in the same time.
  • Faster, clearer claims improve retention and renewals.
  • Better triage reduces leakage on questionable claims.

How much working time can it save?

  • Adjusters spend less time keying data.
  • Underwriters read summaries before full files.
  • Service teams answer fewer routine requests.

Hours saved are roughly tasks per month × minutes per task × share of tasks automated. We fill that in with your own numbers during discovery, so the estimate comes from your operation and not from a generic claim.

Where does the efficiency gain come from?

  • Consistent application of guidelines.
  • Shorter cycle time from notice to settlement.
  • Clear audit trail on every case.

How do we measure success?

We agree a baseline before building, then track:

  • Claim cycle time
  • Straight-through processing rate on simple claims
  • Submissions quoted per underwriter
  • Claims leakage and fraud referral hit rate

Common questions

Does the AI decide on claims or cover?

No. It prepares information and flags issues. Decisions on cover, price and payment stay with authorised staff.

Can it connect to our policy admin system?

Usually through APIs, files or RPA-style integration, depending on what the system exposes. We confirm during discovery.

How do you handle fairness and bias?

We test for inconsistent outcomes, document the logic and keep a human reviewer on adverse decisions. Regulatory requirements vary by market, so your compliance team signs off.
