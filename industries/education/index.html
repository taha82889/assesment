How does automation increase revenue?

  • Higher enrolment conversion from faster admissions responses.
  • Better course completion and retention.
  • New paid programmes created faster.

How much working time can it save?

  • Teachers spend less time on first drafts and routine feedback.
  • Admin teams answer fewer repeat questions.
  • Registrars process fewer documents by hand.

Hours saved are roughly tasks per month × minutes per task × share of tasks automated. We fill that in with your own numbers during discovery, so the estimate comes from your operation and not from a generic claim.

Where does the efficiency gain come from?

  • Quicker response to students and applicants.
  • Consistent course information.
  • Staff time directed to students who need help.

How do we measure success?

We agree a baseline before building, then track:

  • Application-to-enrolment conversion
  • Time to first response for students
  • Course completion rate
  • Instructor hours per course update

How does a project with Smart Logics work?

  1. Discovery. We map your workflow, data and systems, and agree what success means.
  2. Architecture and pilot plan. We choose the smallest useful first release and how it connects to your tools.
  3. Sprint delivery. Working software every sprint, tested on your real data.
  4. Release and iteration. Monitoring, quality checks and improvements after launch.

Common questions

Will the AI do students' work for them?

Design choices matter here. We can configure assistants to explain and guide rather than hand over answers, in line with your academic policy.

Can it support Arabic and English courses?

Yes, with human review of educational content in each language.

How is student data protected?

We limit what is collected, restrict access, and follow your institution's data policy. Rules for minors vary, so we confirm requirements first.
