AI and automation for Retail & E-commerce

Smart Logics builds AI systems for retailers and online stores: computer vision for shelf and store analytics, assistants that answer customer questions, and automation for catalogue, returns and support work. The aim is simple. Sell more from the traffic you already have, and spend fewer staff hours on repeat tasks.

What can Smart Logics offer retailers, e-commerce operators and multi-store chains?

Shelf and store analytics

Cameras and computer vision that detect empty shelves, misplaced stock and queue build-up, then alert staff in time to act.

Shopping and support assistant

An assistant trained on your catalogue, policies and order data that answers questions, recommends products and hands off to a person when it should.

Catalogue and content automation

Bulk product descriptions, attribute cleaning and translation (including Arabic) with a human review step before anything goes live.

Demand and replenishment forecasting

Models that use sales history, seasonality and promotions to suggest order quantities per store or warehouse.

How does automation increase revenue?

  • Fewer lost sales: empty shelves and out-of-stock items get fixed sooner.
  • Better conversion: product pages and recommendations that answer the buyer's actual question.
  • Higher repeat purchase: faster, more accurate service after the sale.

How much working time can it save?

  • Support agents spend time on difficult cases, not order-status questions.
  • Merchandising teams stop writing and fixing listings by hand.
  • Store staff stop doing manual shelf checks.

Hours saved are roughly tasks per month × minutes per task × share of tasks automated. We fill that in with your own numbers during discovery, so the estimate comes from your operation and not from a generic claim.

Where does the efficiency gain come from?

  • Orders and returns follow one consistent rule set.
  • New products reach the site faster.
  • Stock decisions rely on data instead of memory.

How do we measure success?

We agree a baseline before building, then track:

  • Out-of-stock rate and time to restock
  • Conversion rate on assisted vs unassisted sessions
  • Tickets per 100 orders and share resolved without an agent
  • Hours per new SKU from supplier file to live listing

Common questions

Can you add AI to our existing Shopify, Magento or custom store?

Usually yes. We connect through APIs and webhooks to your platform, catalogue and order system, and we confirm the integration approach in a discovery call before any build starts.

Do shelf cameras work with the CCTV we already have?

Often, if image quality, angle and lighting are good enough. We assess a sample of your footage first, and tell you honestly if new cameras are needed.

Will the assistant invent answers about products or prices?

It should only answer from your approved catalogue and policies, and pass anything else to a person. We test this against real customer questions before launch.
