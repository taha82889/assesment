AI and automation for Sports & Recreation

Smart Logics builds computer-vision systems for sports venues: automatic match capture, player and ball tracking, highlight clips and booking-linked analytics. Venues get more value from every court or pitch, and players get footage and stats without anyone operating a camera.

Automated match capture and highlights

Fixed cameras record sessions and cut highlight clips automatically for players and coaches.

Player and performance analytics

Tracking, event detection and per-player summaries.

How does automation increase revenue?

  • Paid recordings, highlights and analytics subscriptions for players.
  • Higher court utilisation through data-driven pricing and promotions.
  • Sponsorship and advertising inventory on shared clips.

How much working time can it save?

  • No manual filming or editing.
  • Coaches stop tagging video by hand.
  • Front desk spends less time on schedule queries.

Hours saved are roughly tasks per month × minutes per task × share of tasks automated. We fill that in with your own numbers during discovery, so the estimate comes from your operation and not from a generic claim.

Where does the efficiency gain come from?

  • Every session is captured consistently.
  • Utilisation is measured, not guessed.
  • Content is ready for sharing sooner.

How do we measure success?

We agree a baseline before building, then track:

  • Court or pitch utilisation rate
  • Share of players buying recordings or subscriptions
  • Hours of editing replaced
  • Highlights shared per week

How does a project with Smart Logics work?

  1. Discovery. We map your workflow, data and systems, and agree what success means.
  2. Architecture and pilot plan. We choose the smallest useful first release and how it connects to your tools.
  3. Sprint delivery. Working software every sprint, tested on your real data.
  4. Release and iteration. Monitoring, quality checks and improvements after launch.

Recording people raises consent and privacy duties, especially for minors. Policy sign-off is needed before launch.

Common questions

Which sports can you support?

We scope sport by sport. Tracking quality depends on the game, camera placement and venue lighting, so we test on your site first.

Do players have to consent to being recorded?

Rules differ by country and venue. We build consent, signage and retention controls into the system, and your legal owner confirms the policy.

Can this work indoors and outdoors?

Yes, but outdoor lighting and weather change the setup. We assess it during discovery.
